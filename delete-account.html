App Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your Card Vault or V.E.N.T account and associated app data by contacting Forged By Fire Software LLC.

Creative QR: Local Data and Feedback

Creative QR has no app account to delete. Use Reset to clear the current design, and Settings > Clear profile & presets to remove saved profile details and website presets. Delete saved images through Saved files or their export folder. Remove settings backups, copied images and shared files separately.

To request access, correction or deletion of feedback already sent, email forgedbyfiresoftware@gmail.com. Include Creative QR, the approximate date and the reply email or enough of the message to locate it. Do not include QR passwords or other private contents. We may need more information to identify an anonymous submission.

We aim to handle identifiable requests within 30 days. Clearing local app data does not remove submitted feedback. See Creative QR privacy for retention and limited records that may need to be kept.

Latter-day Lens: Local Data and Feedback

Latter-day Lens has no app account to delete. Notes, bookmarks, reading position, font size, theme choice, and personal music file references are saved on your computer. The app includes local scripture texts, but no music recordings. Use the app's delete controls for notes and bookmarks, and Settings > Open data folder to manage saved workspace and music-reference files. Exported JSON or Markdown backups are files you choose and must be managed separately.

To request access, correction, or deletion of feedback already sent, email forgedbyfiresoftware@gmail.com. Include Latter-day Lens, the receipt number or approximate submission date, and the reply email or enough of the message to locate it. Anonymous feedback may be difficult to identify.

Clearing local app data does not remove submitted feedback. See our privacy policy or contact support with questions about feedback data.

How To Request Deletion

  1. Email your deletion request to forgedbyfiresoftware@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: App Account Deletion Request.
  3. Name the app and include the username, profile name, or email address used with the account.
  4. Do not include passwords, unlock codes, private keys, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Data Deleted

When your request is verified, we will delete or de-identify account data associated with the named app, including:

  • Profile or account records
  • Saved chat threads and archive records
  • Uploaded attachments tied to the account
  • Voice recordings and transcripts tied to the account, when identifiable
  • Card Vault synchronized collection, deck, and preference records tied to the account
  • App session records that are no longer needed for security, support, or legal reasons

Data We May Keep

We may keep limited records when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling, payment records, abuse prevention, or backup integrity. Backup copies may remain for a limited period before normal backup rotation removes them.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. If we need more information to verify the account, we will contact you using the email address from your request.
