Our Commitment

Forged By Fire Software LLC builds privacy-first tools. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and what control you have.

Plain-English Summary

  • Most desktop apps are local-first - PC Purifier, Master Generator, and The Forge are designed to keep their main files and settings on your device
  • Creative QR for Windows, Android and Apple devices - QR creation, scans, saved designs, selected center images and exports are handled locally. Optional feedback sends the category, message, optional reply email, app version and platform label to us. The Apple edition is currently in App Store review. Read the Creative QR privacy details
  • Creative Image Tools - Selected images and metadata are processed locally and are not uploaded. Optional Windows and Android feedback sends only the message, category, optional reply email and app version when you press the send button. Read the dedicated Creative Image Tools privacy policy
  • CarTrev Towers - Local profiles and complete saves stay on your Windows PC. You may optionally create or link a passwordless commander to share a chosen public name, supported achievements and leaderboard values across devices. Read the CarTrev Towers privacy details
  • Scrapline - The signed Android 1.0 Store candidate stores campaign progress, salvage, equipment and settings locally. It has no player account, advertising, analytics, billing or cloud sync. Optional feedback sends only the category, message, optional reply email and app version when you choose Send. Read the Scrapline privacy details
  • CarTrev Towers Pocket - The Android game and browser trial keep battlefield progress, tower state and preferences on the device or in that browser. Android offers optional passwordless online records with a player-chosen public commander name; no advertising or gameplay analytics is used. Read the CarTrev Towers Pocket privacy details
  • Latter-day Lens for Windows, Android and macOS - The scripture library, reading position, bookmarks, notes, appearance settings, backups and exports stay on your device. The Android version prepared for Google Play disables Android cloud backup and device transfer for its private app data. No account, advertising, analytics or cloud sync is used. Optional feedback sends only the message, category, optional reply email, app version and platform label when you choose Send. Read the Latter-day Lens privacy details
  • ForgeFlix - The Xbox app is a private local-media client for an owner-controlled home-network server. It does not include movies, sell data, or send your local library to Forged By Fire Software LLC. Read the ForgeFlix privacy details
  • Some features need permissions - Creative QR for Android and Card Vault may ask for camera access, and some apps store local data so they can remember your work
  • Some features use the Internet - Master Generator uses the public TreV gateway for TreV responses and downloads image packs when you choose them; Card Vault uses our Card Vault service and card-data providers for search, price data, accounts, sync, Coach, and optional scan reports
  • Optional uploads are real uploads - If you submit a Card Vault scan report, support email, or project request, that content is sent to us or to the service named in this policy
  • Payments are handled by outside processors - Website purchases and donations are handled by PayPal. In-app purchases, Coach credit packs, and subscriptions are handled by the applicable app store, such as Google Play, Microsoft Store, or Apple, not by a Forged By Fire payment form
  • Anonymous website measurements - The public company pages use first-party, privacy-focused analytics to count visits, page views, product and Store-link interest, campaign sources, coarse device categories, country, performance, errors, scrolling and time ranges. We do not assign a persistent visitor ID or store visitor IP addresses in the analytics database
  • We do not sell your data - We do not use advertising networks, ad profiles, or cross-site tracking scripts
  • You can request deletion - Account deletion instructions are available at Delete Account, or you can email forgedbyfiresoftware@gmail.com

What We Collect

Website (cmforgedbyfire.com)

  • First-party aggregate analytics - On the public company pages, we record the requested page path; anonymous event type such as a confirmed page view, product link, Store link, download, blog entry, scroll milestone, missing-page request or performance measurement; referring website domain; campaign tags you followed; country code supplied by Cloudflare; coarse mobile, tablet or desktop category; browser and operating-system family; broad time-on-page range; and website performance or error category. We do not record text that you type, complete referring URLs, or unrelated URL-query contents
  • No persistent visitor profile - The analytics system does not use tracking cookies, an account identifier, browser fingerprinting, advertising identifiers, session replay, or a persistent visitor ID. Events cannot be used by us to reconstruct one named person's browsing history. The site uses local storage only when a visitor asks it to remember an analytics exclusion preference
  • No IP addresses in our analytics database - Cloudflare and hosting providers necessarily process connection addresses to deliver and protect requests. Our analytics collector does not copy those addresses into analytics records. A one-way, process-local digest may be held briefly in memory to limit automated abuse, and a separately secured one-way digest may be used to exclude the owner's network. Neither is used to identify visitors or measure repeat behavior
  • Public visit counter - The footer displays an approximate count of confirmed visits since anonymous counting began. Missing pages do not count as visits. The site also waits for an active, focused page or real browser interaction before confirming a visit and suppresses recognizable automated browsers. These safeguards reduce preview and crawler traffic but cannot identify every automated request, so the number remains approximate
  • Retention - Individual anonymous event rows are automatically removed after 90 days. Daily totals and anonymous rollups, which do not contain IP addresses or visitor identifiers, may be retained to compare long-term website and product interest
  • Your choice - You can disable these first-party measurements on your current browser by opening this analytics exclusion link. The exclusion preference remains on that browser until its site data is cleared. You can re-enable measurements with this re-enable link
  • Card Vault web app boundary - The separate Card Vault web application is not instrumented by this company-page analytics script. Card Vault service activity follows the Card Vault terms below
  • Support email - The support form opens your email app. Your reply details, message, screenshots, or attachments are sent only when you choose to send the email
  • Project request form - When you press Send project request, the form sends the contact details, project category, goals, requested features, budget and timeline, and any optional project information you entered through our Cloudflare-protected HTTPS gateway to a private Project Requests section in App Master. Do not enter passwords, payment information, private keys, health records, or other sensitive information. We use the submission only to evaluate the request, ask follow-up questions, and discuss a possible scope, price, or timeline. Submitting it does not create a contract or guarantee acceptance
  • Project request storage and anti-spam - Project requests are stored in the private App Master database on Forged By Fire's service computer. The public gateway accepts this form only from cmforgedbyfire.com, limits request size, uses a hidden spam-trap field, and applies short-lived network rate limiting. Network addresses used for that rate limiting are converted to process-local one-way values held only in memory and are not added to project-request records
  • Service providers - GitHub Pages hosts the public pages and may process normal web request details such as IP address, user agent, request time and requested URL for security and reliability. Cloudflare delivers and protects the analytics and project-request endpoints. Google provides the Gmail inbox used only when you separately choose email support

Android center-crops the selected image and stores a local app-private copy for the current design and any saved design that uses it; removing the image or deleting the saved design removes its corresponding private copy. The original image remains wherever you selected it and must be managed separately.

Android exports are saved as PNG files in Pictures/Creative QR; Android 9 or earlier may request storage permission when you save there. QR images contain the information you entered, so anyone who scans a contact, message or Wi-Fi code may read that encoded information. Copy places the decoded contents on the device clipboard. Printing or sharing hands the selected PNG and, on Android, its encoded text to the printer, application or recipient you choose. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

The form does not attach QR contents, scans, center images, saved designs, device identifiers or detailed device information. Anything you type into the message is included. Failed or unsent feedback is not automatically retried.
  • Use and storage - Feedback is stored in our private App Master database so we can respond when an email is supplied, investigate problems and improve Creative QR. It is not a public comment and is not used for advertising. Network addresses are used temporarily in memory to limit abusive submissions. Our service and hosting providers may process connection details for delivery and security; see the service-provider section below.
  • Your choices - Feedback and Android camera access are optional. Windows provides controls to reset the current design, clear profiles and presets, and manage saved exports. Android lets you delete individual saved records, clear the saved gallery and remove a custom center image; clearing Creative QR's app storage or uninstalling removes remaining private app data. Exported PNGs, clipboard contents and copies you shared must be removed separately. These actions do not delete feedback already submitted. To request feedback access, correction or deletion, contact forgedbyfiresoftware@gmail.com. Include Creative QR, the platform, approximate submission date, and enough of the message or reply email to locate it. An anonymous message may be difficult to identify. See Creative QR data deletion and Data Retention below.
