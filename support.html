CarTrev Towers Windows Support

CarTrev Towers 1.0.1 is a local-first tower defense game for Windows. Profiles, saves, progress and the leaderboard are stored on your PC; no CarTrev online account or password is required.

Use Settings > Send feedback to report a problem or suggestion from the game. A reply email is optional, and profile names, saves, progress, statistics, settings and computer details are not attached. If feedback cannot send, the draft stays in the form while it remains open. You can also select CarTrev Towers Windows in the email form below. Include the app version, battlefield, wave, what you expected and what happened. Do not send private files or unrelated personal information.

Deleting a local profile in Settings removes that defender and its progress from the game. See the links below for full privacy and local-data instructions.

Creative QR Windows and Android Support

Creative QR is available free for Windows on Microsoft Store and for Android on Google Play.

Windows creates customizable QR codes and printable cards. Android creates and saves QR designs and also includes an on-device camera scanner that starts only when you choose to scan.

Use the app's feedback control or select Creative QR Windows or Creative QR Android in the form below. Include the platform, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Leave passwords, Wi-Fi credentials, and private QR contents out of your message or screenshots.

Latter-day Lens Windows, Android and Mac Support

Latter-day Lens is available free for Windows on Microsoft Store and Android on Google Play. The Apple edition is currently in App Store review.

Your scripture reading, word search, meaning search, notes, bookmarks and appearance settings stay on your device. On Android, export a study backup before clearing app storage or uninstalling if you want to preserve your notes and bookmarks; automatic Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled.

Use the app's Feedback control or select the exact Latter-day Lens platform in the form below. Include the app version, device, operating-system version, what you expected and what happened. Do not include private notes, scripture-study details, personal music files or unrelated personal information.

Creative Image Tools Support

Creative Image Tools is a released, free and ad-free image utility for Windows and Android. Image processing stays on the device, originals are preserved, and no account is required.

Windows includes optional feedback in the lower sidebar, and Android includes it under About. It sends only your message, selected category, optional reply email and app version when you press the send button. Images, filenames, paths, queue contents, settings and device identifiers are not attached.

Select Creative Image Tools in the email form below, or use the dedicated support page for troubleshooting and privacy requests.
