CarTrev Towers Windows Support

CarTrev Towers 1.0.1 is a local-first tower defense game for Windows. Profiles, saves, progress and the leaderboard are stored on your PC; no CarTrev online account or password is required.

Use Settings > Send feedback to report a problem or suggestion from the game. A reply email is optional, and profile names, saves, progress, statistics, settings and computer details are not attached. If feedback cannot send, the draft stays in the form while it remains open. You can also select CarTrev Towers Windows in the email form below. Include the app version, battlefield, wave, what you expected and what happened. Do not send private files or unrelated personal information.

Deleting a local profile in Settings removes that defender and its progress from the game. See the links below for full privacy and local-data instructions.

Creative QR Windows Support

Creative QR v0.1 creates QR codes and printable cards on your Windows PC. It is available free on Microsoft Store.

In the app, choose Feedback beside the version number to report a problem or suggest an improvement. A reply email is optional. Sending needs an internet connection; QR creation and PNG exports work offline.

If feedback cannot send, the message stays in the form while the app remains open. You can also select Creative QR Windows in the email form below. Please include the app version, what you expected and what happened. Leave passwords and private QR contents out of your message or screenshots.
