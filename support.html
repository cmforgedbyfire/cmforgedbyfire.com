Creative QR Windows Support

Creative QR v0.1 creates QR codes and printable cards on your Windows PC. It is available free on Microsoft Store.

In the app, choose Feedback beside the version number to report a problem or suggest an improvement. A reply email is optional. Sending needs an internet connection; QR creation and PNG exports work offline.

If feedback cannot send, the message stays in the form while the app remains open. You can also select Creative QR Windows in the email form below. Please include the app version, what you expected and what happened. Leave passwords and private QR contents out of your message or screenshots.
