CarTrev Towers

Two brothers. Four realms. One line to hold.

Carson and Trevour enter every Windows battlefield together—first as the brothers at the heart of an illustrated campaign, then as included hero towers. Follow their war against Kalz Mahan and the Forsaken through 16 victories, or play the separate touch-first Android edition and free phone-ready browser trial.

  • Complete Windows storySeven opening scenes, story progress after every victory, and a Chapter One finale.
  • Two included heroesCarson brings arcane anti-air power; Trevour brings the hardest single ground strike.
  • Four distinct realmsFoundry, medieval, monster, and space battlefields with specialized towers and enemies.
  • Local progressionSeparate defenders retain campaigns, saves, achievements, statistics, and appearance on the PC.

Scrapline

Your mech. Your next mission.

Enter a broken combat zone in a machine you build over time. Pilot the versatile Scrapper or earn the heavier Bulwark, recover loose scrap under fire, and protect the cargo that funds your next permanent workshop upgrade.

  • Four ways to fightThree 11-wave campaign missions plus Endless Crucible survival.

EllBen Defense

Build a home. Hold the frontier.

Begin with a house, one follower, and a frontier that will not defend itself. EllBen Defense connects open 3D tower-defense battles to a persistent countryside where victories earn credits, attract residents, improve your home, and turn empty plots into a growing kingdom.

  • From house to kingdomDevelop cottages, gardens, workshops, watchtowers, and fourteen permanent plots.
  • Free-placement defenseBuild Bolt, Bastion, Ember, and palisade defenses on clear battlefield ground.
  • Tinker flight crewsHire and improve up to three repair flyers, then direct their work during battle.
  • Claim the wildlandsDefeat seeded creature expeditions to extend the countryside road network.
Windows PC · In DevelopmentNative Godot 3D Prototype

The medieval realm is playable now. Art, balance, campaign story, and future realms remain in development; this is not yet a signed installer or Store release.

Humans

One people. Worlds beyond imagining.

Humans is an original offline real-time strategy game about humanity building a future across hostile realms. Gather timber and ore, establish a settlement, train an army, survive raids, expand beyond the starting ground, and adapt to worlds with different dangers and rules.

  • The ThornwildA playable monster-realm foundation with workers, construction, fog, patrols, raids, and siege units.
  • Build and commandGather resources, place structures, queue production, form armies, and establish a frontier camp.
  • Break the riftCross the fords, destroy monster dens, weaken incoming raids, and clear the realm objective.
  • Worlds aheadThe Shattered Moon and Drowned Reach establish the direction for future distinct realms.
Windows PC · In DevelopmentOffline Single-player

Humans is a development prototype, not a signed installer or Store release. Difficulty, art, realm identity, and the larger campaign are still being developed.
