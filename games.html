Humans

One people. Worlds beyond imagining.

Humans is an offline 3D real-time strategy game about establishing a lasting human foothold in worlds that play by different rules. Gather finite resources, build a working settlement, train realm-specific armies, hold against raids, destroy dens and rifts, and lead your people through The Thornwild, The Shattered Moon, and The Drowned Reach.

  • Three playable 3D realmsCross Thornwild rivers, harvest Moon crystals, and manage diver oxygen beneath the Drowned Reach.
  • Build a living footholdGather finite resources, raise farms and production buildings, bridge rivers, repair damage, and grow toward a 100-person population cap.
  • Command the expeditionUse formations and attack-move orders across melee, ranged, and siege units with realm-specific weapons and enemies.
  • Raise a ChampionTrain a persistent Lion Champion, advance through 20 levels, and equip six inventory pockets with gear found across the realms.
  • Fortify the frontierUpgrade Watchtowers into Attack and Cannon Towers, establish distant camps, repel raids, and break enemy dens and rifts.
  • Play locallyExplore 112-by-112 maps with fog of war, perspective camera controls, local saves, autosaves, and backup recovery.

Scrapline

Your mech. Your next mission.

Enter a broken combat zone in a machine you build over time. Pilot the versatile Scrapper or earn the heavier Bulwark, recover loose scrap under fire, and protect the cargo that funds your next permanent workshop upgrade.

  • Four ways to fightThree 11-wave campaign missions plus Endless Crucible survival.
  • Two combat mechsMaster the Scrapper and unlock the armored Bulwark.
  • Component damageProtect each arm, manage incoming fire, and trigger Pulse when surrounded.
  • Offline progressionWorkshop improvements and campaign progress remain directly on the device.

EllBen Defense

Build a home. Hold the frontier.

Begin with a house, one follower, and a frontier that will not defend itself. EllBen Defense connects open 3D tower-defense battles to a persistent countryside where victories earn credits, attract residents, improve your home, and turn empty plots into a growing kingdom.

  • From house to kingdomDevelop cottages, gardens, workshops, watchtowers, and fourteen permanent plots.
  • Free-placement defenseBuild Bolt, Bastion, Ember, and palisade defenses on clear battlefield ground.
  • Tinker flight crewsHire and improve up to three repair flyers, then direct their work during battle.
  • Claim the wildlandsDefeat seeded creature expeditions to extend the countryside road network.
