AI Projects in Production

These are working systems under active production development. Availability is listed honestly for each project.

OTTO

Local-first Windows desk assistant

OTTO is being built to help everyday people understand, operate, and troubleshoot their technology. Its working desktop foundation can explain computer, network, storage, startup, device, and Windows status in plain language, then prepare a reviewable troubleshooting path.

Read-only diagnostics come first. Anything that would change settings, files, drivers, apps, or the system must be presented as a clear plan and approved by the user. OTTO also supports local transcription of user-selected audio and video files into text and VTT captions.

Windows Local AI Approval-gated Active development

Availability: Working private development build. Public Store packaging is still in progress.

V.E.N.T

Reflection and journaling companion

V.E.N.T helps people talk something through, keep private reflection threads, save useful moments, notice patterns, use grounding tools, and choose one small next step. Profiles, archives, Heart memories, search, attachments, and optional voice input give each person control over what they keep.

A dedicated safety layer is designed to respond proportionally, preserve the user's agency, avoid diagnosis or unsupported certainty, and direct credible danger toward real-world help. V.E.N.T is not a therapist, crisis line, emergency service, or replacement for professional or trusted personal support.

The Forged By Fire Approach

Honest Boundaries

AI Implemented Inside Our Apps

TreV and Coach are not demos or stand-alone chat boxes. They are implemented in the products where their context is useful.

TreV

Campaign companion in Master Generator

TreV helps game masters turn generated material into a connected campaign. It can suggest encounter ideas, event hooks, campaign ties, expansion notes, and table direction using the result and campaign context the user chooses to send. Core generators and campaign vaults remain local; TreV uses the Forged By Fire HTTPS service only when the user asks for a response.

Coach

Collection-aware guidance in Card Vault

Coach works with a user's Magic: The Gathering or Pokemon collection and decks. It combines structured deck checks with AI guidance to review legality, surface useful synergies and combinations, suggest improvements, recommend owned cards, and build decks around the selected format and goal. Coach runs through the Card Vault service and uses only the relevant collection, deck, format, and request context sent for that analysis.
