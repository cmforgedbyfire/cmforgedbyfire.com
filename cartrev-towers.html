Fight Through Chapter One

A story after every victory

Begin with a seven-part illustrated opening, then unlock a character-led story scene after each of the 16 battlefields as the Scottites reclaim the Four Realms.

Two included hero towers

Carson strikes ground and air with arcane magic while Trevour lands the brothers' hardest single blow against ground enemies. Each hero costs 250 match coins and may be placed once per map.

The Chapter One finale

Win all 16 battlefields to unlock an eight-part illustrated finale. The victory the brothers expect becomes the beginning of the road they must follow next.

Four distinct realms

Fight through Foundry, Medieval, Monster, and Space battlefields. Each realm has its own towers, enemies, bosses, artwork, and tactical character.

Persistent progression

Earn experience, career credits, tower licenses, cosmetic skins, achievements, and evolving profile frames. Each local defender keeps separate progress.

Control every wave

Place and upgrade towers, counter ground and flying enemies, survive heavy aircraft and double-boss finales, pause the battle, adjust speed, or use auto-play between waves.

Local Profiles and Records

CarTrev Towers keeps profiles, appearance, campaign progress and complete saves in the app's Windows data folder. No password or online identity is required for normal play.

Version 1.0.6 keeps achievements, detailed career summaries, match history and leaderboards on the Windows PC. It does not create an online game identity, synchronize gameplay records or publish scores. The release has no advertising, behavioral analytics or in-game payments. Optional feedback remains separate and sends only what the form describes when you choose Send.
