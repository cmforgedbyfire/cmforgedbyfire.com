Local Profiles and Privacy

CarTrev Towers 1.0.1 runs its game service on your own PC. Local profile names, appearance, progress, saves, achievements and leaderboard records stay in the app's Windows data folder. No password or CarTrev online account is required.

This release has no advertising, analytics, payments, cloud sync or remote gameplay connection. Optional in-app feedback uses the internet only when you choose Send feedback and includes only your message, category, optional reply email and app version. Your profile and game data are never attached. Microsoft may process Store account, download, installation and diagnostic information under Microsoft's own privacy terms.
