Local Profiles and Optional Online Records

CarTrev Towers keeps profiles, appearance, campaign progress and complete saves in the app's Windows data folder. No password or online identity is required for normal play.

You may optionally create or link a passwordless commander to share a chosen public name, supported achievements and leaderboard values across Windows and Android. Complete saves are not uploaded. The release has no advertising, behavioral analytics or in-game payments. Optional feedback remains separate and sends only what the form describes.
