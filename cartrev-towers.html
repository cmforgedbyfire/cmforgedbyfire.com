Local Profiles and Optional Online Records

CarTrev Towers keeps profiles, appearance, campaign progress and complete saves in the app's Windows data folder. No password or online identity is required for normal play.

Version 1.0.5 lets you optionally create or link a passwordless commander to privately synchronize supported achievements and detailed normal-campaign career summaries across Windows and Android. Public leaderboard participation is a separate choice and remains off until you enable it. Complete saves, exact tower placements and detailed Endless records are not uploaded. The release has no advertising, behavioral analytics or in-game payments. Optional feedback remains separate and sends only what the form describes.
