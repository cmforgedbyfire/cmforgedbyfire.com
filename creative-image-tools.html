Four Focused Tools

Compress

Create JPEG copies under an exact KB or MB ceiling, with a result reported for every selected image.

Resize

Resize copies by pixels, percentage, or practical presets while preserving aspect ratio and preventing unwanted enlargement.

Convert

Create copies using formats verified on each platform instead of promising encoders that may not exist on the device.

Clean Metadata

Inspect privacy categories and remove selected location, device, author, capture-date, or editing-history details from a new copy.

Private by Design

  • Images and their metadata are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Originals are preserved; outputs use collision-safe names rather than silently replacing files.
  • No advertising, analytics SDK, cloud image service, subscription, watermark, or required account.
  • The Windows and Android apps connect only when you deliberately send optional feedback. They never attach images, filenames, paths, queue contents, settings, or device identifiers.
