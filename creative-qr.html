What You Can Make

Seven QR types

Website/link, contact card, email draft, text-message draft, phone number, Wi-Fi connection details or plain text. Enter your own information and save reusable website presets.

Colors and custom icons

Choose QR ink and paper colors, card palettes, backgrounds, frames and glow. Try different QR patterns and corners, or add a PNG, JPG or WebP center icon.

Preview, save and print

Preview the QR-only image with an adjustable size. Export PNGs, print a card, and open, rename or delete saved images from the app.

Cards for Different Uses

Start with the standard 900 × 1080 card. Other presets include square, sticker, flyer, story, widescreen, 4 × 6, Letter and A4. These define PNG dimensions; physical print size depends on your printer's paper and scaling settings.

Scan your final image with a phone before sharing or printing. Decorative patterns, colors and center icons can affect readability. Creative QR v0.1 creates codes; it does not include a camera scanner.

Local Work, Optional Feedback

QR creation and exports work offline. Your saved profile and presets stay on your device, and exported images contain the information you entered. Creative QR does not add a hosted redirect or an expiration date to your codes; the linked website or other destination still needs to remain valid.

Use Feedback beside the version number if you want to send a message to the developer. Sending uses the internet and includes your message, category, optional reply email, app version and Windows platform. QR contents, icons and saved profiles are not attached automatically.
