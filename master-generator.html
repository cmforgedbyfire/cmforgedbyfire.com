What You Get

Campaign Generators

Generate practical table-ready ideas for NPCs, inns, settlements, shops, monsters, encounters, treasure, magic items, dungeons, regions, and names.

TreV Campaign Companion

Ask TreV for encounter ideas, event hooks, campaign ties, expansion notes, and table direction through the public TreV gateway.

Downloadable Visual Packs

Save map and magic item card packs for offline table use after downloading them in the app.

Campaign Vault

Save useful results into a local campaign vault, then load them later without needing an account or cloud sync.

Generator Packs

Shift generators into classic open fantasy, weird fantasy, horror, low-magic grit, city intrigue, wilderness travel, naval trouble, or planar strangeness.

Export and Import

Export your campaign vault as a JSON file and import it again when you want a backup or a transfer.
